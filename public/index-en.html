I

Neptune commands. Vulcan makes.

They are two different things and it is worth not confusing them. NEPTUNO is the framework: it observes, controls, orders and commands. VULCANO is the work: what is done by hand, line by line.

And it is not a forced metaphor. In the myth, Vulcan forged Neptune's trident. The workshop makes the weapon. The god wields it.

BRISECOM The company. Signs and answers. The parent brand.
NEPTUNO The resilience framework. Observes, orders and commands.
VULCANO The work. The forge. The craft.
LAIA The engine. A tool, not a god.
II

What it is

Is

  • The seal that the work was done by hand. A person signs it
  • The brand of the services: audit, deployment, integration
  • The craft: method, workmanship, human judgement, line-by-line review
  • What the client writes on an invoice: “craft work”

Is not

  • It is not the network. The network belongs to NEPTUNO
  • It is not a product. It is the how
  • It does not replace BRISECOM: it is what BRISECOM does
  • It is not a brand that needs explaining in every meeting
III

The argument is not modesty

We use the same tools as anyone else. The good ones; the standard ones. There is no secret in the steel.

What changes is what happens after the tool.

Someone reads every finding. Checks it. Discards it if it is a false alarm. Looks at it again when it does not add up. And signs the report with their name.

Anyone can own the tool. Time and judgement, no. This is not a romantic stance: it is what you are buying.

IV

Four blows

The craft, in the order it is done.

I

Look

With open eyes and no hurry. Before touching anything, you understand what is there.

II

Doubt

What looks like a finding may be noise. You check before writing it down.

III

Discard

Half of what comes out is worthless. Saying so is also part of the job.

IV

Sign

With a name and a surname. The one who answers is a person, not a logo.
